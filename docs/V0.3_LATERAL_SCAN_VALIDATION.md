# SkyScrub V0.3 Lateral Facade Scan Validation

## Objective

Validate controlled lateral movement along a facade while continuously
maintaining autonomous facade standoff using PX4 Offboard control.

## Test configuration

Facade standoff:

- Target range: 2.00 m
- Range deadband: +/- 0.08 m
- Maximum approach speed: 0.20 m/s
- Maximum retreat speed: 0.15 m/s

Lateral scan:

- Commanded lateral displacement: 0.80 m
- Maximum lateral speed: 0.12 m/s
- Lateral position deadband: +/- 0.05 m
- Standoff stabilization time: 2.0 s
- Endpoint hold time: 2.0 s

## Test sequence

The controller performed the following sequence:

1. Hold the initial airborne position.
2. Request PX4 Offboard mode.
3. Approach the facade.
4. Acquire the 2.00 m standoff region.
5. Stabilize at the facade.
6. Start the lateral scan.
7. Execute the lateral displacement.
8. Return toward the original lateral scan center.
9. Enter the COMPLETE state.
10. Continue holding facade standoff after scan completion.

## Initial state

Before controller activation:

- PX4 mode: Hold
- Vehicle armed: yes
- Failsafe: no
- Altitude: approximately 2.53 m
- Initial facade range: approximately 5.66 m
- Heading good for control: true
- Dead reckoning: false

## Lateral scan result

The state machine successfully progressed through the lateral scan and
eventually reached:

state=COMPLETE

This confirms that the controller completed its configured scan sequence
and returned to the completion state.

After completion, the vehicle continued maintaining facade standoff.

Representative post-scan measurements included facade ranges such as:

- 2.071 m
- 2.079 m
- 2.067 m
- 2.051 m
- 2.038 m
- 2.005 m
- 2.000 m
- 2.010 m
- 2.030 m

The controller therefore remained close to the 2.00 m target after the
lateral maneuver.

A brief measurement of approximately 2.085 m exceeded the configured
+0.08 m deadband by approximately 0.005 m.

The range controller responded with a small forward correction of
approximately +0.021 m/s and returned the vehicle toward the desired
standoff region.

## Altitude stability

During the completed scan and subsequent hold, reported NED altitude
remained approximately within:

-2.53 m to -2.56 m

No significant vertical deviation was observed during the lateral test.

## Final state

After the test, control was returned to PX4 Hold mode and the vehicle
was landed/disarmed.

Final PX4 commander state:

- Disarmed
- Navigation mode: Hold
- User intended navigation mode: Hold
- Failsafe: no

## Result

PASS

SkyScrub V0.3 successfully demonstrated a controlled lateral facade
scan while maintaining closed-loop facade standoff in simulation.

## Current limitations

- The lateral test covered only one 0.80 m excursion and return.
- Vertical facade movement has not yet been validated.
- Multi-row facade scanning has not yet been implemented.
- Window-by-window coverage logic has not yet been implemented.
- The 2.00 m facade distance is still a provisional simulation target.
- The front sensor is a simulated narrow GPU lidar/range surrogate.
- Wind, facade turbulence, water spraying, and cleaning forces are not
  included in this test.
