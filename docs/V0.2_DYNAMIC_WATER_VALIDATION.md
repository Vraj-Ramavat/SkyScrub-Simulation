# SkyScrub V0.2 Dynamic Water Depletion Validation

## Objective

Validate that SkyScrub remains stable while onboard water mass decreases
during one uninterrupted PX4 SITL + Gazebo flight.

## Simulation configuration

- PX4 SITL
- Gazebo Harmonic 8.15.0
- EFT E610P geometry
- Hobbywing X6 propulsion approximation
- Initial planning mass: 26.893 kg
- Dry planning mass: 18.893 kg
- Initial water payload: 8.000 kg
- Water representation: 32 detachable packets
- Packet mass: 0.250 kg
- Navigation mode: Hold
- Hover target: approximately 2.5 m
- Initial MPC_THR_HOVER: 0.558

> NOTE: The dry mass still contains a provisional 2.0 kg accessory
> installation allowance. These are V0.2 planning masses, not final
> measured physical BOM masses.

## Dynamic depletion results

| Water Remaining | Vehicle Mass | Average Motor Command | Result |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 8.00 L | 26.893 kg | 0.55836 | PASS |
| 7.75 L | 26.643 kg | 0.55337 | PASS |
| 6.00 L | 24.893 kg | 0.52811 | PASS |
| 4.00 L | 22.893 kg | 0.49784 | PASS |

## Single-packet proof

Water packet 00 was detached successfully during flight.

Mass transition:

26.893 kg -> 26.643 kg

Average motor command:

0.55836 -> 0.55337

The aircraft remained near the 2.5 m hover target.

Navigation mode remained Hold.

Failsafe remained inactive.

## Full depletion test

All 32 water packets were detached during the same uninterrupted flight.

Water transition:

8.000 L -> 0.000 L

Vehicle mass transition:

26.893 kg -> 18.893 kg

As vehicle mass decreased, PX4 automatically reduced the required motor
command while maintaining altitude.

Throughout the test:

- Navigation mode remained Hold
- No failsafe occurred
- Local position remained valid
- Heading remained valid for control
- Altitude remained approximately 2.5 m
- Motor demand progressively decreased with vehicle mass

## Final empty-payload state

At 0 L water remaining:

- Vehicle mass: 18.893 kg
- Average motor command: approximately 0.43033
- Altitude: approximately 2.524 m
- Vertical velocity: approximately 0 m/s
- Navigation mode: Hold
- Failsafe: no

This closely matched the independently validated static EMPTY configuration.

## Landing

The aircraft completed a normal landing after the full depletion test.

PX4 reported:

- Landing detected
- Disarmed by landing
- Failsafe: no

## Conclusion

PASS.

SkyScrub V0.2 maintained stable altitude control while its simulated onboard
water payload decreased from 8 kg to 0 kg during one uninterrupted flight.

The test demonstrates automatic controller compensation for the changing
vehicle mass in the current V0.2 Gazebo planning model.

## Simulation limitation

Water depletion is approximated using 32 discrete detachable 0.25 kg packets.

This provides real changes in simulated carried mass during flight, but it is
a discrete approximation of continuous liquid discharge and does not yet
model fluid slosh or continuously changing tank inertia.
