# SkyScrub V0.3 Vertical Facade Scan Validation

## Objective

Validate controlled vertical facade movement while continuously
maintaining autonomous facade standoff using PX4 Offboard control.

## Test configuration

Facade standoff:

- Target range: 2.00 m
- Range deadband: +/- 0.08 m
- Maximum approach speed: 0.20 m/s
- Maximum retreat speed: 0.15 m/s

Vertical scan:

- Commanded vertical displacement: 0.80 m
- Maximum vertical speed: 0.12 m/s
- Vertical position deadband: +/- 0.05 m
- Standoff stabilization time: 2.0 s
- Upper-position hold time: 2.0 s

PX4 local coordinates use NED convention.

Therefore:

- More-negative Z = climb
- More-positive Z = descend

## Test sequence

The controller performed the following sequence:

1. Begin from stable airborne Hold mode.
2. Request PX4 Offboard mode.
3. Acquire the 2.00 m facade standoff.
4. Stabilize at the facade for 2 seconds.
5. Command a 0.80 m vertical climb.
6. Hold at the upper position.
7. Return toward the original altitude.
8. Enter the COMPLETE state.
9. Continue holding facade distance after scan completion.

## Initial state

Controller reference:

- Starting Z: approximately -2.562 m NED
- Initial planned climb target: approximately -3.362 m NED
- Yaw: approximately 1.746 rad

After standoff stabilization, the vertical reference was refreshed.

Final climb target used for the scan:

- Start Z: approximately -2.554 m NED
- Target Z: approximately -3.354 m NED
- Commanded displacement: 0.80 m

## Standoff acquisition

PX4 entered Offboard mode successfully.

At Offboard activation:

- Facade range: approximately 1.979 m
- Range error: approximately -0.021 m
- Vertical offset: approximately 0.000 m
- Lateral drift: approximately 0.000 m

The controller reported:

STANDOFF ACQUIRED - stabilizing

and then:

STANDOFF STABLE - starting vertical climb

The facade range at the beginning of the climb was approximately
1.99 m.

## Standoff performance

During the active vertical maneuver, the measured facade range
remained approximately within:

1.927 m to 1.992 m

This remained within the configured standoff band of:

1.92 m to 2.08 m

The facade-distance controller therefore remained effective while
the vehicle moved vertically.

## Lateral stability

During the active vertical maneuver, lateral drift remained small.

Representative values were approximately within:

-0.042 m to +0.019 m

No significant sideways deviation was observed during the scan.

## Post-scan hold

After entering COMPLETE state, the vehicle continued hovering while
maintaining facade standoff.

Representative facade ranges remained close to the 2.00 m target,
generally around approximately 1.95 m to 1.98 m.

## Landing and final state

The test ended with a normal PX4 landing.

PX4 reported:

- Landing detected
- Disarmed by landing
- Navigation mode: Hold
- User intended navigation mode: Hold
- Failsafe: no

## Result

PASS

SkyScrub V0.3 successfully demonstrated controlled vertical facade
movement while maintaining closed-loop facade standoff in simulation.

## Current limitations

- This test covered only one 0.80 m vertical excursion and return.
- Horizontal and vertical motion have not yet been combined into one
  multi-row facade coverage path.
- Window-by-window coverage logic has not yet been implemented.
- Cleaning actions are not yet coupled to scan position.
- The 2.00 m facade distance remains a provisional simulation target.
- The front sensor remains a simulated narrow GPU lidar/range surrogate.
- Wind, facade turbulence, spray forces, and real cleaning loads are
  not included in this test.
